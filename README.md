# World News — Monday 23 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026, for make.fun's
`news-image-20261123` bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123

Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Sum up 23 November's world news in one image" bounty, $100 in USDC.

## Stories

1. **COP31 ends with no deal** (Climate) — the UN climate summit in Antalya closes
   without agreement over fossil fuels.
2. **Ukraine sinks Russian warship off Libya** (War) — naval drones sink the corvette
   Merkury at Benghazi; a cargo ship is also hit.
3. **Nvidia brings back Jensen Huang** (Business) — the board reinstates Huang as CEO;
   shares jump about 30%.
4. **Indonesia factory fire kills 24** (Disaster) — blaze at a sweets factory in
   Banten; dozens still missing.
5. **Russian strikes kill 15 near Kyiv** (War) — missiles hit the Kyiv region; a boy
   is pulled alive from rubble.
6. **BepiColombo reaches Mercury** (Science) — spacecraft enter orbit around Mercury
   after an eight-year journey.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (or `/output/entry.png`). It runs with no network;
the image is in this repository.

`entry.png` is a JPEG (1024x1024, 226,162 bytes) under the name `entry.png`, as the
bounty allows (PNG or JPEG, told apart by first bytes).
